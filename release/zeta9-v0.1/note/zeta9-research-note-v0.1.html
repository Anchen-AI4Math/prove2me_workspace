Status statement (read first). This note does not claim a proof of the irrationality of ζ(9). No such proof exists in the material described here, conditionally or otherwise. The purpose of this version (v0.1) is to make public the concrete constructions, the local arguments that are proved (in writing, with the caveats stated), the computations that were actually run and archived, and the open problems that remain. Every substantial statement carries a status tag: R a written proof exists and no error was found on review; R* proved conditional on a named standard input (e.g. the prime number theorem); M proved only modulo another claim in the same corpus; N verified numerically (in places with interval arithmetic) on an explicitly finite range, with no uniformity; H heuristic, not a proof step; OPEN needed but absent. “Not formalized” is reported as a fact about the state of formal verification, not as a logical gap in a written proof; conversely, no statement is claimed to be formally verified unless it is.

1. Aim, scope, and provenance

These notes record a two-day research effort (24–25 September 2026) on the irrationality of ζ(9), carried out in a local workspace and partially mirrored into a private mission on a crowdsourced formalization platform. The effort comprises nine research rounds (round 1 and rounds 2–9), a roadmap dependency graph (40 named nodes) that redirected the programme after round 9, and a platform layer containing eight theorem-level formal proofs with abstract statements. A full proof-state audit of the corpus was prepared on 25 September 2026 and is included in the source archive as provenance; the present note is the public-facing distillation of that material, with three factual corrections applied (Section 8).

What is published here. (i) Two concrete families of constructions producing rational linear forms in ζ(9) (Part I); (ii) the exact algebra, integrality theorems, valuation formulas and a certified analytic upper bound around them; (iii) finite certified computations with archived artifacts and reproduction instructions; (iv) the later constructions and the current roadmap with its open nodes (Part II); (v) a canonical list of what is missing.

What is not claimed. No irrationality statement; no eventual nonvanishing of any linear form along an infinite sequence; no decay of any integer primitive form; no asymptotic for any true primitive denominator; no formal verification of the note-proved layer. Finite computations are reported as finite computations: a value certified at a tested parameter is never presented as a statement about infinitely many parameters, and the first success inside a discrete scan is never presented as a minimal threshold or a final answer.

2. Conventions

Rising Pochhammer $(x)_r=x(x+1)\cdots(x+r-1)$. Harmonic sums $H_j^{(r)}=\sum_{i=1}^{j}i^{-r}$, $H_0^{(r)}=0$. $v_p$ is the $p$-adic valuation; $v_p(n!)=\sum_{k\ge1}\lfloor n/p^k\rfloor$ (Legendre). $d_n=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\dots,n)$, so $\log d_n=\psi(n)$ is Chebyshev's $\psi$ (all prime powers; an earlier draft wrote $\vartheta$, which was corrected in the source notes). $\{x\}=x-\lfloor x\rfloor$. $\zeta(s)=\sum_{k\ge1}k^{-s}$.

Part I — The first-round routes (24 September 2026)

3. The main construction and its exact algebra

For positive even $n$ and $0\le m\le 3n/14$ (the grid uses $m=\alpha n$, $\alpha\in\{0,\tfrac1{28},\tfrac1{14},\tfrac3{28},\tfrac17,\tfrac5{28},\tfrac3{14}\}$), define $$R_{n,m}(t)=\frac{(n!)^3}{(m!)^{14}}\,\frac{\bigl[(t-m)_m\,(t+n+1)_m\bigr]^{7}}{(t)_{n+1}^{3}}, \qquad L_{n,m}=\frac1{6!}\sum_{k\ge1}R^{(6)}_{n,m}(k).$$ $R_{n,m}$ has poles of order $\le3$ exactly at $0,-1,\dots,-n$, zeros of order $7$ at $1,\dots,m$ and $-n-m,\dots,-n-1$, and $R_{n,m}(t)=O(t^{-2})$; hence $L_{n,m}$ converges absolutely. [R] Partial fractions $R_{n,m}(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{n}\bigl(\tfrac{C_j}{(t+j)^3}+\tfrac{D_j}{(t+j)^2}+\tfrac{E_j}{t+j}\bigr)$ have the closed form $$C_j=(-1)^{m+j}\tbinom{n}{j}^{3}\tbinom{j+m}{m}^{7}\tbinom{n-j+m}{m}^{7}\in\mathbb Z,$$ with $D_j=C_ju_j$, $E_j=\tfrac{C_j}{2}(u_j^2+v_j)$, where $u_j=10H_j-10H_{n-j}-7H_{j+m}+7H_{n-j+m}$ and $v_j=10H_j^{(2)}+10H_{n-j}^{(2)}-7H_{j+m}^{(2)}-7H_{n-j+m}^{(2)}$. [R] The coefficient of $t^{-1}$ at infinity vanishes ($\sum_jE_j=0$), and reflection $R_{n,m}(-n-t)=-R_{n,m}$ for even $n$ gives $\sum_jD_j=0$; consequently the $\zeta(7)$- and $\zeta(8)$-coefficients of the six-derivative sum die exactly and $$L_{n,m}=A_{n,m}\zeta(9)+B_{n,m},\qquad A_{n,m}=28\sum_{j=0}^n C_j,\quad B_{n,m}=-\sum_{j=0}^{n}\bigl(E_jH_j^{(7)}+7D_jH_j^{(8)}+28C_jH_j^{(9)}\bigr)\in\mathbb Q. \quad \text{[R]}$$ Worked example (recomputed independently for the audit, to the full precision of the archived certificate): at $(n,m)=(2,0)$, $C=(1,-8,1)$, $D=(-\tfrac92,0,\tfrac92)$, $E=(12,-24,12)$, $A=-168$, $B=176.228515625$, and $L=7.89110563021818797780073476895\ldots$

4. Integrality and $p$-adic arithmetic

Write $d=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\dots,n+m)$ and $G=\gcd_{0\le j\le n}|C_j|$. Two integerization theorems are proved in writing:

  • Elementary. $t_{n,m}A_{n,m},\,t_{n,m}B_{n,m}\in\mathbb Z$ with the raw multiplier $t_{n,m}=2d^{9}/G$. [R]
  • Improved (headline). For $m\le 3n/14$, $t^{\sharp}_{n,m}A_{n,m}\in\mathbb Z$ and $t^{\sharp}_{n,m}B_{n,m}\in\mathbb Z$ with $t^{\sharp}_{n,m}=d_n^{9}/G$; the two multipliers are consistent, $t_{n,m}/t^{\sharp}_{n,m}=2(d/d_n)^9$. [R, written] The written proof removes the surface factor $2$ via integral binomial Taylor coefficients and lowers the harmonic denominator bound from $n+m$ to $n$ by a carry argument on the top prime-power level. Caveat: the written proof is a sketch; the carry bookkeeping is its least-protected step. Its conclusion (not the proof) was independently re-verified on five parameter pairs up to $n=224$: $G$, $d$ recomputed from scratch match the archived records, $t^{\sharp}A,t^{\sharp}B\in\mathbb Z$, and $t^{\sharp}/m_{\mathrm{prim}}$ equals the archived integer. [N, five cases]
  • Minimality. If $tF\in\mathbb Z[X]$ for a positive rational $t$, then $t/m_{\mathrm{prim}}\in\mathbb Z_{>0}$: no clearing convention can shrink an already-computed primitive form. [R]

The valuations are exact: $v_p(C_j)=3v_p(n!)+7v_p((j+m)!)+7v_p((n-j+m)!)-10v_p(j!)-10v_p((n-j)!)-14v_p(m!)$, and $G\mid C_0=\pm\binom{n+m}{m}^7$. [R] For large primes there is an exact formula: if $p^2>n+m$ and $n\ge2m$, then with $r=n\bmod p$, $s=m\bmod p$, $$v_p(G)=7\cdot\mathbf 1_{\,r+2s\ge 2p-1}. \qquad \text{[R; re-checked on 216 parameters / 1{,}951 prime terms, 0 failures, N]}$$ Small primes contribute $o(n)$, and the floor-boundary discrepancy contributes at most $7\log(n+2m+1)=o(n)$ [R*, PNT]. Along $m=\alpha n$, $$\lim\frac{\log G}{n}=\Gamma(\alpha),\qquad \Gamma(\alpha)=7\int_{1}^{\infty}\frac{\mathbf 1_{\{x\}+2\{\alpha x\}\ge2}}{x^{2}}\,dx,\qquad \lim\frac{\log t^{\sharp}_{n,m}}{n}=9-\Gamma(\alpha), \quad \text{[R*, PNT]}$$ with $0\le\Gamma(\alpha)\le14\alpha$ [R]. Rational interval enclosures of $\Gamma(\alpha)$ (period decomposition, outward rounding on a $10^{-32}$ grid, explicit tail bound) give, for the six grid values, $\Gamma\in[0.14171563,0.14171565],\dots,[0.85210820,0.85210822]$; all six intervals were reproduced independently. [N] Boundary: these are asymptotics of multipliers that were proved valid. Since $t^{\sharp}$ is valid, $m_{\mathrm{prim}}\mid t^{\sharp}$, so $9-\Gamma(\alpha)$ is an upper bound for $\lim\log m_{\mathrm{prim}}/n$ — not the cost of the primitive form. The measured finite gaps $g_{n,m}=\tfrac1n\log(t^{\sharp}/m_{\mathrm{prim}})$ at $n=224$ are $0.86,0.71,0.71,0.67,0.62,0.74$ and show no sign of vanishing; their limit is unknown. Statements of the form “cost $\ge 9-14\alpha$” are lower bounds for an upper bound of the true cost and carry no consequence by themselves.

5. The contour identity and the analytic upper bound

Let $K_7(z)=\sum_{k\in\mathbb Z}(z-k)^{-7}=\tfrac1{6!}\tfrac{d^{6}}{dz^{6}}(\pi\cot\pi z)$. Two exact closed forms are used interchangeably in the corpus (both re-verified numerically for this release against a direct truncated sum, agreement to $10^{-18}$ at 50-digit working precision): $$K_7(z)=\frac{\pi^{7}\bigl[302\cos(\pi z)+57\cos(3\pi z)+\cos(5\pi z)\bigr]}{360\,\sin^{7}(\pi z)} \;=\; i\,\frac{(2\pi)^{7}}{720}\sum_{\ell\ge1}\ell^{6}q^{\ell}, \qquad q=e^{2\pi iz},\ \Im z>0,$$ where $\sum_{\ell\ge1}\ell^{6}q^{\ell}=\dfrac{q\,(1+57q+302q^{2}+302q^{3}+57q^{4}+q^{5})}{(1-q)^{7}}$ and the Eulerian numerator coefficients $1,57,302,302,57,1$ sum to $720=6!$, which is what gives the principal part $(z-k)^{-7}$ at each integer. (In the trigonometric form the numerator coefficients $302,57,1$ and the denominator are both $360$; the two forms are consistent. See Correction C-I, Section 8.) [R]

Exact contour identity. For $c\in(m,m+1)$, in particular $c=m+\tfrac12$, $$L_{n,m}=-\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_{c-i\infty}^{c+i\infty}R_{n,m}(z)\,K_7(z)\,dz. \qquad \text{[R]}$$ The orientation sign was re-checked independently by direct numerical contour integration at $(n,m)=(2,0)$: $I=-49.5812789531890297\,i$ and $-I/(2\pi i)=L$ to 28 digits. [N, one case]

Phase. With $z=nw$ and principal branches in the upper half-plane, Stirling summation gives the analytic phase $$\Phi_\alpha(w)=-14\alpha\log\alpha+10w\log w+7(w{+}1{+}\alpha)\log(w{+}1{+}\alpha) -7(w{-}\alpha)\log(w{-}\alpha)-10(w{+}1)\log(w{+}1),$$ in which the $n\log n$ coefficient cancels exactly (factorial prefactor $3-14\alpha$; products $14\alpha-3$), and $$\log|R_{n,m}(nw)|=n\,\Re\Phi_\alpha(w)+O_\alpha(\log n)$$ at leading order — i.e. $\tfrac1n\log|R_{n,m}(nw)|=\Re\Phi_\alpha(w)+o(1)$. [R; scale corrected, see C-II] A shifted-Riemann-sum argument ($\delta=1/(2n)$, shift of the line $\Re w=\alpha$ by $\delta$ costing $O_\alpha(\log n)$, uniformly in $y\ge0$) upgrades this to a uniform estimate $\log|R_{n,m}(m+\tfrac12+iny)|\le n\Re\Phi_\alpha(\alpha+\delta+iy)+O_\alpha(\log n)$; the written argument is a sketch with untracked constants, sufficient for a limsup but not for any finite-$n$ statement. [M, sketch] Combined with the contour identity, a compact Laplace bound and an exponential tail (valid because $14\alpha-3\le0$), $$\limsup_{n\to\infty,\ m=\alpha n}\frac{\log|L_{n,m}|}{n}\ \le\ \sup_{y\ge0}H_\alpha(y), \qquad H_\alpha(y)=\Re\Phi_\alpha(\alpha+iy)-2\pi y. \quad \text{[M]}$$ This is an upper bound on the exponential rate of $|L|$. The certified bounds on the tested parameter grid are positive and therefore do not establish decay. Upper bounds alone do not establish nonvanishing or a matching asymptotic formula.

The derivative has the exact form $H'_\alpha(y)=\tfrac{3\pi}{2}-10\arctan\tfrac{y}{\alpha}-7\arctan\tfrac{y}{1+2\alpha}+10\arctan\tfrac{y}{1+\alpha}$, with $H'_\alpha(0)=\tfrac{3\pi}{2}$ and $H'_\alpha(\infty)=-2\pi$ [R; re-derived, N-checked to $10^{-40}$]. $H''_\alpha$ times the positive product of the three quadratics is a quadratic in $y^2$ whose constant coefficient is negative, linear coefficient positive, and leading coefficient exactly $3-14\alpha$; hence $H'$ has exactly one positive zero, the global maximizer of $H_\alpha$ [R, shape] [N, per $\alpha$; degenerate leading coefficient at the endpoint $\alpha=3/14$, handled by the linear root]. 7. The secondary (Gram) route

Independently, a positive-weight functional for odd arguments (inherited from an earlier local study of odd zeta values) gives, at $s=9$: $$w_9(y)=\tfrac{2(2\pi)^8}{8!}\,y^{9}\sum_{\ell\ge1}\ell^{8}e^{-2\pi\ell y},\qquad \mu_X(t^{e})=(-1)^eB_{2e+2}\tfrac{(2e+9)!}{8!(2e+2)!},\qquad \mu_X\bigl((t+j^2)^{-1}\bigr)=j^{8}\bigl(X-H_j^{(9)}\bigr)-\tfrac18+\tfrac1{2j}.$$ The functional and the sign of the $+1/(2j)$ term are inherited from the earlier notes (gamma integral, Euler's even-zeta formula, Hermite's formula for Hurwitz zeta); they were not re-derived in this effort. [R, inherited] With $D_a(t)=\prod_{j=1}^{a}(t+j^{2})$, $h=K-N$, and $G_{ij}(X)=\mu_X\!\bigl(D_N(t)^{2q}t^{i+j}/D_K(t)\bigr)$, $\Delta_K(X)=\det G(X)$: at $X=\zeta(9)$ the matrix $G$ is positive definite (an integral of a square against a positive weight), and a square Vandermonde factorization gives $\deg_X\Delta_K=h$; hence $\Delta_K(\zeta(9))>0$ for every $K$. [R, inherited] This is the only genuine nonvanishing statement in the corpus, and it is positivity at finite $K$ — it says nothing about size relative to the denominator.

On the arithmetic side the corpus has a moment-integrality/rank lemma (for $p\ge11$ the moments are $p$-integral for $e<4p-5$; at $e=4p-5$ von Staudt–Clausen gives valuation exactly $-1$; [R*, named inputs]) and a rank bound $\min\bigl(h,[K+6N-4p+4]_+\bigr)$ adapted from the $s=7$ pattern. [M] Caveats: the index shift was not re-derived and differs by one unit from the $s=7$ pattern (flagged as an unverified adaptation, not as an error; no conclusion depends on it), and this is explicitly not a determinant valuation theorem. A structural remark records that the leading $K^2$ potential-theoretic terms are unchanged from $s=7$ to $s=9$ while the prime-by-prime integerization cost is not transferable. [H]

Finite Gram evidence: 18 records at $K=40$ plus 3 promoted profiles at $K=80$; all 21 values $>1$; $r_K=\log P_K(\zeta(9))/K^{2}$ rises from $K=40$ to $K=80$ in all three promoted profiles ($1.343774\to1.429423$; $1.387844\to1.432339$; $1.514384\to1.623551$). Regression controls on the two earlier odd zeta values reproduce the archived screening values with matching coefficient hashes; one control value is the only record below one. Assignment dual certificates covering all denominator primes for the best profile at both scales give an extra cost over the primitive multiplier of $\approx1.690479$ ($K=40$) and $1.817305$ ($K=80$) after an integer Newton change of basis; the primitive polynomial is unchanged. [N, finite; explicitly not used as an asymptotic bound]

8. Corrections applied in this release (v0.1)

C-I (R19 coefficient sum). The internal audit text states that the Eulerian numerator of $\sum_\ell \ell^6 q^\ell$ “sums to $360$”. The coefficients are $1,57,302,302,57,1$ and sum to $720=6!$; $720$ is exactly the value required for the principal part $(z-k)^{-7}$, and the displayed formula with the prefactor $(2\pi)^7/720$ is correct as written. The separate trigonometric form $\pi^7[302\cos\pi z+57\cos3\pi z+\cos5\pi z]/(360\sin^7\pi z)$ has numerator coefficients summing to $360$ against a denominator $360$ and is self-consistent. Both forms were re-verified numerically against a direct sum (agreement $10^{-18}$ at 50-digit precision). Impact: none on any formula, script or archived constant — the only script using these identities uses the trigonometric form with the correct $360$; the audit prose is wrong, the mathematics is not. The corrected value $720$ is used throughout this note.
C-II (R20/R21 phase scale). The audit's item R20 writes the Stirling phase as “$\log|R|=\tfrac1n\Re\Phi_\alpha(w)+O_\alpha(1)$”, inconsistent with its own R21 and with the source note. The correct scale is $\log|R_{n,m}(nw)|=n\,\Re\Phi_\alpha(w)+O_\alpha(\log n)$, equivalently $\tfrac1n\log|R|=\Re\Phi_\alpha(w)+o(1)$; the exact $n\log n$ cancellation ($3-14\alpha$ vs $14\alpha-3$) is consistent only with the $n\,\Re\Phi$ scale. Impact: none downstream — the uniform estimate and the limsup bound quoted in the audit use the correct scale; only the one-line R20 statement was mis-scaled.
C-III ($f_*$ vs $\tau$ inequality direction). With $\tau=2641/250=10.564$ and $f_*$ the maximum of the round-5–7 phase function, the certified statement (rational atanh-series bounds, independently reproduced) is $$-10.564155 < f_* < -10.564,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad f_* < -\tau,\ \ -f_* > \tau,$$ so the proved decay exceeds the required threshold by a margin of $-f_*-\tau = 10.5641538\ldots - 10.564 \approx 1.54\times10^{-4}$ per unit $n$. The audit's roadmap paragraph (“with $-f_* < \tau$ proved”) and its gap item G8.7 (“the proved statement is $-f_* < -\tau$”) both state the wrong direction; the source roadmap states $f_* < -\tau$ correctly, and the numerical margin in the audit is correct. This note uses the correct direction.

Classification hygiene enforced throughout. (i) Smallness at finitely many tested parameters, nonvanishing at finitely many tested parameters, finite certificates, and statements about infinite sequences are four different things and are never conflated here. (ii) “Not formalized” is reported as a formalization-status fact, never as a logical gap in a written proof; conversely no statement is called verified unless it is. (iii) The eight platform theorems are abstract; they are never cited as evidence that the concrete ζ(9) constructions satisfy their hypotheses. (iv) First successes inside discrete scans (e.g. the five-point cubature weights, §10) are reported as scan results, not as located thresholds.

10. The roadmap dependency graph (state of 25 September 2026)

After round 9 the programme was redirected into an explicit roadmap working with the $p=9$, $m=n$, $\deg W\le4$ family of rounds 5–7. Objects: the form matrix $F_n$ (rows $u^0,\dots,u^4$, $u=t(t+n)$; columns $(B,A_3,A_5,A_7,A_9)$); the column selection $S$ onto $(B,A_9)$; the inverse image $E_n=SF_n^{-1}$; the saturated integer kernel basis $K_n$; the common denominator $D_n$ of the primitive two-dimensional image with Smith factors $s_{1,n}\mid s_{2,n}$, $N_n=s_{2,n}/s_{1,n}$, $g_n=\gcd(N_n,d_n^2)$; weights $\operatorname{diag}(1,n^2,n^4,n^6,n^8)$; the weighted area $\Delta_n$; the congruence-lattice first minimum $\mu_n$; the threshold $\tau=2641/250=10.564$ with the certified margin $f_*<-\tau$ (C-III). The key exact identity is $$B_n+\sigma_n=\frac{\log(D_n/s_{1,n})+\log\Delta_n-\log\mu_n}{n},\qquad B_n=\frac{\log(D_n/s_{1,n})+\tfrac12\log\Delta_n-\tfrac12\log g_n}{n},\quad \sigma_n=\frac{\tfrac12\log(g_n\Delta_n)-\log\mu_n}{n}.$$

Node census: 40 nodes, 13 open, 27 note-proved. “Note-proved” means a prose proof exists in a research note and was read in the audit; none has a Lean term or a platform theorem, and the audit is not a substitute for independent verification of each one. The 13 open nodes include the actual target J (there exist $\varepsilon>0$ and infinitely many even $n$ with $B_n+\sigma_n\le\tau-\varepsilon$), the stronger candidates V ($\limsup B_n<\tau$) and S ($\sigma_n\to0$), the arithmetic budget chain VA/VB/VC/VD, the geometric sign nodes T5/T/TS/TG, the endpoint criterion FD, and the critical-scale node CP. The note-proved nodes include the corpus's only equivalence (CM: $\zeta(9)\notin\mathbb Q \iff \lambda_{2,n}=o(T_n) \iff \liminf \lambda_{2,n}Z_n=0$, with $T_n=n^5e^{\alpha n}$, $\alpha=-f_*$), the uniform kernel decay A ($|L_n(w)|\le e^{(-\tau+o(1))n}\sqrt5\|w\|_{n,2}$), the exact volume cancellation Q ($B_n=\frac{\log\Xi_n}{2n}+\frac{\log(N_n/g_n)}{2n}$), the discount identity YD, and the geometric lemmas FQ/GC/TA/FI/FC/FO/XL/TP/SP. The proved reductions are one-directional: $\mathrm{T5}\wedge\mathrm{FQ}\wedge\mathrm{X}\wedge\mathrm{A}\wedge\mathrm{F}\Rightarrow R$ (likewise with T, TS, TG); $\mathrm{CP}+\mathrm{CM}\Rightarrow R$; $V\wedge S\Rightarrow J$; $J\Rightarrow$ CP; $\mathrm{FD}+\mathrm{FC}+\mathrm{FI}\Rightarrow\mathrm{T5}$; $VA+Y\Rightarrow V$; $VB+Y\Rightarrow VA$; $VC+H+Z\Rightarrow VB$; $VD\Rightarrow VA$. Nothing proves any of the 13 open nodes; V and S are not necessary for J.

Proved negative results (with scopes). GO (a rank-2 integer polynomial lattice can meet the dimension/area/shortness data yet show both signs at three of the five points — geometry alone cannot give T5); FM (a rational connection with the same spectral limit, two-step positivity, the same five nodes and exact positive cubature can fail T5 forever — the spectral limit alone cannot give T5); the round-7 spectral obstruction and the round-8/9 filter obstructions (§9). All four refute classes of arguments; none is a no-go theorem for the route itself.

The numerical distance. V needs $\limsup B_n<\tau$; the proved split $\frac{\log\Xi_n}{2n}<\gamma=\frac14\log(3\,711\,015\,000)\approx5.50864282$ plus the open $VA$ ($\limsup\frac{\log(N_n/g_n)}{2n}<\tau-\gamma\approx5.05535718$) would give it. The tightest proved uniform estimate in that direction is $\limsup(\mathcal E_n^{\mathrm{mid}}-\mathcal U_n)/n<22.337$, against a requirement of $0.61071437$ (node VD): a gap of more than a factor of 30, in the direction where the proved bound is not small enough. Five finite parameters cross ($B_n+\sigma_n\approx10.4933,10.4423,10.4767,10.1416,10.2703$ for $n=12,24,48,96,192$, each below $\tau$ by $0.07$–$0.42$); finite crossings imply nothing, as the roadmap itself states.

The FQ/T5 activation finding (new in the audit). The five designated samples are $k_{n,j}=\lfloor nx_*+j\sqrt{n/a}+\tfrac12\rfloor$, $j=-2..2$, with $x_*=1.00297646575489808$ and $a=330.65816828687$ (reproduced independently to full displayed precision). Because $\sqrt{n/a}\approx0.055\sqrt n$ ($1/\sqrt a = 0.05499337\ldots$) while $n(x_*-1)\approx0.00298n$, the five points become distinct and all exceed $n$ only for $n\gtrsim1686$ (recomputed on a discrete set of test values: degenerate at $n\le1400$; first fully valid at $n=2000$ among the tested values — no minimality over all $n$ is claimed, and a proof for all $n\ge n_0$ would need a separate argument with controlled error and floor/rounding estimates). Every finite parameter ever used in the effort ($n\le384$) lies below this threshold, so no finite regression in the corpus exercises FQ or T5 at all. Quantitatively, solving the exact five-point system for the true kernel moments gives weights that are not all positive at $n=1000$ and $n=2000$ and are positive at $n=6000$ and $n=20000$ (Gaussian-moment control at the same nodes is positive at every tested $n$ and reproduces $(1/12,1/6,1/2,1/6,1/12)$ exactly; the true moment deviation decays like $n^{-1/2}$). These finite observations do not locate a threshold for positivity at all sufficiently large $n$; the first success in this discrete scan is not a located threshold, and the archived computations were not re-run for this release. The mechanism is sound and FQ's “eventually” remains plausible as stated; the mission's own finite certificate covers the limit constant ($330.658<a<330.659$), not the convergence. [N, finite scan]

11. The platform (formalization) layer

The local graph was partially wired into a private mission on a crowdsourced formalization platform (identifiers deliberately omitted from this public note). Read back from local receipts: an open root theorem ($\zeta(9)\notin\mathbb Q$); two accepted root decompositions (SKETCH_ACCEPTED, one one-form and one two-form) whose obligations require exponentially small nonzero or independent integer forms at a pinned exponential rate. No claim of strict logical strengthening over the root is made here; establishing such strengthening would require checking the precise coefficient, construction, and indexing constraints of the formal statements, which was not done for this release. (A child merely asserting “there exist small integer forms” would be equivalent to the root once a criterion is available — a disguised restatement, not a decomposition; the decompositions were designed to avoid at least that failure mode.) And eight theorem-level formal proofs, all compiled locally with Lean 4.33.1 / Mathlib and accepted by the platform: two general reductions, three abstract cores (volume cancellation, prime budget, prime discount), and three wired lemmas (a one-form irrationality criterion, a Taylor-sign lemma, an exact-cubature lemma). Crucially, all eight have abstract statements. The concrete objects of the roadmap ($F_n$, $K_n$, the Smith data, the weighted areas, the congruence-lattice minima) have no Lean definitions; consequently the accepted abstract proofs do not discharge the corresponding concrete local nodes, and no arithmetic arrow ($V\leftarrow VA,Q,X$, etc.) was published — deliberately, since a parent with free parameters cannot import a closed child theorem without a definition layer. None of the 27 note-proved nodes is Lean-checked. Nothing in this section is evidence that the concrete ζ(9) constructions have been formally verified.

12. Open problems (canonical closure list)

The items below include mathematical obligations for several alternative routes and, separately, formalization tasks. They are not asserted to be jointly necessary for an irrationality proof. A complete rigorous written proof does not require prior formalization.

12.1 Mathematical obligations

  1. C1. An asymptotic (or matching lower bound) for the true primitive multiplier $m_{\mathrm{prim}}$ of the Part-I family.
  2. C2. A lower bound for $|L_{n,m}|$, e.g. a phased saddle main term with non-cancellation (including the logarithm-branch issue in $\Phi'_\alpha(w)+2\pi i=0$).
  3. C3. $A_{n,m}\ne0$ on the Part-I range (never asserted; certified $|P|>1$ does not imply it).
  4. C4. The assembly $0<|P_{n,m}(\zeta(9))|\le e^{-cn}$ along an infinite sequence.
  5. C5. The Gram-branch package: uniformity of the $O(K\log K)$ terms, an independently certified potential constant (the value $M=-1329/200$ used in the earlier local notes is imported and not independently certified; its literature source was not re-verified for this release), and the ζ(9) normalization cost.
  6. C7. One of the geometric nodes T5/T/TS/TG/FD (equivalently CP at the critical scale of CM).
  7. C8. One of the arithmetic nodes J or V$\wedge$S (hence VA, VB, VC or VD), where the proved uniform estimate still stands at $22.337$ against a requirement of $0.61071437$.
  8. C9. A uniform statement over an infinite parameter set in any of the above — the single ingredient missing in all nine rounds.

12.2 Formalization tasks (engineering, not mathematical prerequisites)

  1. C6. Lean definitions for $F_n$, $K_n$, the Smith data, the weighted area and the congruence-lattice minima — without them the roadmap's obligations cannot even be stated faithfully on the platform.
  2. C7†. Formalization of the note-proved geometric layer (the formalization half of the original C7).
  3. C10. Formal verification of the note-proved layer (27 roadmap nodes plus the Part-I proofs); today only eight abstract platform theorems exist.

13. Limitations and evidence boundaries

  • No proof in either direction. The material cannot be combined into a proof that ζ(9) is irrational, nor that it is rational, nor that this family of constructions fails. The two structural reasons: on the main route the analytic side supplies only upper bounds for $|L|$ while the arithmetic side controls only denominators (the inequality needed points the other way, and the denominator controlled is not the primitive one); in the roadmap every closing statement is a for-all/for-infinitely-many statement while the evidence base is five-scale finite certificates. This describes the present state of these particular routes; it is not a claim that any route of this type must first obtain an exact asymptotic for the true multiplier or a quantitative lower bound for $|L|$.
  • Status tags are load-bearing. R means “a written proof exists and no error was found on review” — review here includes re-derivation of some items and scripted re-checks of others, but not machine verification. R* items depend on named standard inputs (PNT via $\psi(x)\sim x$; Legendre's formula; von Staudt–Clausen; Euler's even-zeta formula; Borcea–Brándén Theorem 2). M items are sketches or depend on other corpus claims (R21's untracked constants; R35's unverified index shift). N items are finite. H items are not proof steps.
  • Written proofs with flagged weak points. R8 (improved integerization) is a sketch whose conclusion — not proof — was re-verified on five parameters; R21's uniformity constants are untracked; R35's index shift is an unverified adaptation; the Gram functional (R31/R32) is inherited, not re-derived in this effort.
  • Finite evidence is finite. All certified values ($|P|>1$ on 21+21 samples, five-scale crossings of $\tau$, the $b>10^{422}$ divisor bound at $n=192$, the cubature-weight scan) are statements about the tested parameters only.
  • Computations. Interval-certified objects are the archived Arb balls and exact rationals; displayed decimals are presentational. Ordinary floating-point computations in the notes are not strict interval certifications. No experiment was re-run for this release beyond the small verification checks described in §8 and archived transcripts; where no run record exists, no reproduction is claimed.
  • Literature. Only two external references are load-bearing and both are identified with checkable sources (below). The external ζ(5) construction used as a regression control in the internal notes, and the imported potential constant $M=-1329/200$, were not re-verified for this release and are not relied upon by any statement in this note. Claims from informal discussions about other odd zeta values are not used as sources.
