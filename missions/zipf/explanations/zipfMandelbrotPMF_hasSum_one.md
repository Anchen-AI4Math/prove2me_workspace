For $q\geq0$ and $s>1$, the infinite Zipf–Mandelbrot distribution has total mass $1$:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\frac{1}{C(k+q)^s}=1,\qquad
C=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{1}{(n+q+1)^s}.
$$

The translated $p$-series criterion proves that the defining series for $C$ is summable. Every term is nonnegative and the term at $n=0$ is positive, so $C>0$. Dividing the summable series by this constant gives sum $C/C=1$. Shifting the index from $n$ to $k=n+1$ identifies its terms with the distribution. The omitted rank-zero term is zero by definition, so restoring it does not change the sum.
