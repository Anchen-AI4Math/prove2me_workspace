For every support rank $1\leq n\leq N$ and every real $s$,

$$
\frac{\operatorname{zipfPMF}(N,s,1)}{\operatorname{zipfPMF}(N,s,n)}=n^s.
$$

The assumptions imply $N\geq1$, so $H_{N,s}>0$. They also imply $n>0$, so its real power is positive. The rank-one weight is $1$, and both masses share the same nonzero normalizer. Cancellation gives

$$
\frac{1/H_{N,s}}{(1/n^s)/H_{N,s}}=n^s.
$$
