For $N\geq1$ and every real exponent $s$, the finite Zipf distribution satisfies

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{N}\operatorname{zipfPMF}(N,s,k)=1.
$$

The normalizing constant is positive: every summand in $H_{N,s}$ is nonnegative, and the rank-one summand is exactly $1$. On the interval $1\leq k\leq N$, the definition of the distribution is its unnormalized weight divided by that constant. Thus

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{N}\operatorname{zipfPMF}(N,s,k)
=\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{N}k^{-s}}{H_{N,s}}
=\frac{H_{N,s}}{H_{N,s}}=1.
$$
