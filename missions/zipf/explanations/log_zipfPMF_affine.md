For a support rank $1\leq k\leq N$ and every real exponent $s$,

$$
\log\operatorname{zipfPMF}(N,s,k)=-s\log k-\log H_{N,s}.
$$

Both $k^s$ and the normalizing constant are positive: the first follows from $k>0$, and the second follows from the rank-one contribution to the harmonic sum. The logarithm of a quotient and the logarithm of a positive real power therefore give

$$
\log\frac{1/k^s}{H_{N,s}}
=\log1-\log(k^s)-\log H_{N,s}
=-s\log k-\log H_{N,s}.
$$
