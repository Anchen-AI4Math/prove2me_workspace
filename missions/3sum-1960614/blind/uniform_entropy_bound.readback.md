# Read-back of ThreeSum1960614.uniform_entropy_bound

For every real number $t$ such that $0 \le t \le \frac12$, the strict inequality
$$
\frac{-t\log t-(1-t)\log(1-t)}{\log 2}
<
\frac{2790503}{10000000}
\left(
1+\frac{785174904t+72447023t^2+24044466t^3+12022459t^4}{100000000}
\right)
$$
holds, where $\log$ is the real natural logarithm and its total-function value at zero is $\log 0=0$, so the term $0\log 0$ is zero. The only hypothesis on $t$ is the inclusive interval condition $0 \le t \le \frac12$; both endpoints are covered. At $t=0$ the assertion is $0<\frac{2790503}{10000000}$. At $t=\frac12$ the assertion is $1<\frac{2790503}{10000000}\left(1+\frac{6631298715}{1600000000}\right)$.
