Let $X=n^3D^{-s/2}(\log D+1)^2$. The preprocessing, query, linear construction, query-loop and constant terms in $4ngt$ are each at most $8CX$, by the five proved call-balance inequalities and $w\le n^2/\sqrt D$. Reading the answers contributes at most $8C'X$. Thus the stronger bound $(40C+8C')X$ holds, and nonnegativity of $C$ yields the stated $(64C+8C')X$ bound. All overhead terms are included; the proof uses only standard axioms and the proved call-balance child.
