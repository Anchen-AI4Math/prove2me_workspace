Set p=1/c and q=1/10. Apply log(x) <= x-1 to q/p and (1-q)/(1-p), multiply by p and 1-p, and add. The resulting weighted logarithm inequality gives H(p)+(1-p) log(9) <= log(10). Multiplying by c and using log(9)=2 log(3) shows that the pruned encoding exponent is at most the original encoding exponent A(c).

This independent analytic lemma transfers the strict encoding condition of the certificate to the parameterized pruned program. It does not by itself assert the final algorithmic runtime.
